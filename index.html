About Me

I am Luu Thang (Luu Quoc Thang), a Senior AI Engineer and Project Manager with nearly ten years in software delivery. I am at ease with both the technical and the commercial side of a project: models that must be trained and judged, and plans that must meet a budget and a date.

I have led T&M and Fixed-Price programs, including teams of up to 20 people — planning, risk, quality, and reporting to the customer. As an engineer I design and ship production AI: LLM fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT), RAG and agents, computer vision, and speech (ASR/TTS), mainly with Python and PyTorch, integrated into Java/Spring, React, and cloud services when the product needs it.

I was previously a Technical Leader at FPT Software and a Senior Research Engineer at LG Electronics Vietnam. I still care about architecture, mentoring, and the last details that turn a demonstration into something people can trust.

Alongside company work, I accept a limited number of independent projects for businesses: production AI, websites designed for advertising, mobile applications, and digital-transformation work that teams can run day to day. A short note is always welcome; I will reply with care.
