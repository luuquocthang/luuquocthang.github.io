About Me

I am Luu Thang, a Senior AI Engineer and Project Manager with nearly ten years in software delivery. I am comfortable in both rooms: training and evaluating models, and owning a plan, a budget, and a customer deadline.

I have led T&M and Fixed-Price programs, including teams of up to 20 people — planning, risk, quality, and stakeholder reporting. On the engineering side I design and ship production AI: LLM fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT), RAG and agents, computer vision, and speech (ASR/TTS), mainly with Python and PyTorch, integrated into Java/Spring, React, and cloud services when the product requires it.

Previously I was a Technical Leader at FPT Software and a Senior Research Engineer at LG Electronics Vietnam. I still care about architecture, mentoring, and the last 5% of detail that turns a demo into something users can trust.
