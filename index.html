About Me

I'm Luu Quoc Thang. For nearly a decade I have lived in two worlds at once: the room where a model is trained, and the room where a deadline, a budget and a customer actually matter. That mix is the point — I am a Senior AI Engineer who still thinks like a Project Manager.

I have led T&M and Fixed-Price delivery, including teams of up to 20 people: plan, risk, quality, and the weekly conversation that keeps everyone honest. On the build side I take AI from idea to production — fine-tuning LLMs (LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT), RAG and agents, computer vision, speech (ASR/TTS) — mostly in Python and PyTorch, wired into Java/Spring, React and the cloud when the product needs it.

Before this chapter I was a Technical Leader at FPT Software and a Senior Research Engineer at LG Electronics Viet Nam. Architecture still matters to me. So do people: I like teaching, I learn fast, and I sweat the small details because that is usually where a “demo” becomes something users can trust.
