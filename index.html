About Me

I am Luu Thang (Luu Quoc Thang), a Senior AI Engineer and Project Manager with nearly ten years in software delivery. I am comfortable in both rooms: training and evaluating models, and owning a plan, a budget, and a customer deadline.

I have led T&M and Fixed-Price programs, including teams of up to 20 people — planning, risk, quality, and stakeholder reporting. On the engineering side I design and ship production AI: LLM fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT), RAG and agents, computer vision, and speech (ASR/TTS), mainly with Python and PyTorch, integrated into Java/Spring, React, and cloud services when the product requires it.

Previously I was a Technical Leader at FPT Software and a Senior Research Engineer at LG Electronics Vietnam. I still care about architecture, mentoring, and the last 5% of detail that turns a demo into something users can trust.

I also keep a small independent practice for founders and teams who need a senior owner they can actually ship with. If that is you, a short note is enough.

Independent

If you need one senior person to turn an idea into something users can actually use — an AI feature, an internal tool, or a web product — I take on a small number of those projects myself. Most of them start as a referral. A first conversation is enough to know if we should work together.

What people usually hire me for

AI that has to run

RAG and assistants over your documents, Vietnamese speech (ASR/TTS), computer vision, or fine-tuning a model for a domain task — designed, evaluated, and handed over so it can stay in production.

Web products around the model

The application the user actually opens: React / Next.js, Java / Spring, or FastAPI, with auth, data, and a release a customer can accept. I do not take brochure sites with no product problem behind them.

When delivery is the risk

A stuck AI or software workstream that needs a plan, a scope, and someone accountable through UAT — the same discipline I use on T&M and Fixed-Price programs.

How it works

  1. You write. Problem, who it is for, deadline, and what you already have (docs, data, a repo, or just an idea).
  2. I reply on fit. Yes, a narrower scope, or a clear no. I would rather decline than take work I cannot own.
  3. We ship. One owner from that conversation through something you can put in front of users.

A good fit

Founders, product owners, and small teams who already feel the problem — and want a senior counterpart, not a large vendor process. Confidential by default. I keep only a few engagements at a time so the work stays close.
